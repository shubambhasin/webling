Why does every website want your cookies?

By Ada Pixelton · 6 min read

You open a link. Before a single sentence loads, a banner slides up asking you to accept cookies from 437 partners you have never heard of. You click whatever makes it go away fastest.

Multiply that by every site you visit in a day and you have spent hours of your life negotiating with pop-ups. Researchers call it consent fatigue. Everyone else calls it Tuesday.

Some people have started to fight back in unusual ways.

We value your privacy. So do our 437 partners.
