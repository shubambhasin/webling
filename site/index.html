    Hatch yours.

    Chrome Web Store listing is on its way. Until then it takes about a minute by hand:

    1. Download the extension and unzip it.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked, pick the folder. Name your egg. Choose a vibe (or let fate decide).

    Works in Chrome, Edge, Arc, Brave and friends. Open source on GitHub.

    Nosy pet, private by design.

    • Your browsing never leaves your computer. "Sites explored" is counted locally.
    • Meeting other weblings shares a scrambled page ID, your pet's name and colour. Never the address.
    • No meeting other pets in incognito, on mail, banking or sign-in pages, or anywhere with a password box.
    • Eating is cosmetic: it hides things on your screen. It never clicks, blocks requests or reads forms.
