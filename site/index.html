A tiny pet that lives on the internet.

Hatch a webling and it comes with you to every site. It climbs your headlines, naps on your paragraphs, eats cookie banners and pop-ups, and waves at other people's weblings on the same page.

What it does all day

Lives on the page

It reads the page for itself: every line of text is a ledge to walk on. It hops between paragraphs and drops back in from the top when you scroll.

Eats the annoying stuff

Cookie banners, newsletter modals, "get the app" nags. It chews them away and unlocks the scroll they froze. Hungry pets eat more. Every meal can be spat back out.

Meets other weblings

Reading something popular? Other people's weblings show up on the same page. Boop them and they boop you back.

Grows up

New sites, meals and boops earn xp. Babies become grown-ups, grown-ups become legends with crowns. Share its card when it does.

Private by design

  • Your history stays on your computer. The "sites explored" count is kept locally and never sent anywhere.
  • Meeting other weblings shares a scrambled page ID (a SHA-256 of the page address), plus your pet's name and colour. Never the URL, the title or anything you type.
  • It stays out of private places. No meeting other pets in incognito, on mail, docs, banking and sign-in pages, or on any page with a password box.
  • Eating is only cosmetic. It hides elements on your screen. It never clicks "accept", blocks requests or reads forms.

Get a webling

It's on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, you can install it by hand in about a minute:

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Your egg is waiting.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Arc, Brave and other Chromium browsers.
