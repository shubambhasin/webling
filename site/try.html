Test page

For trying an installed Webling. This page has no pet of its own.

Within a few seconds: your webling drops in, lands on these lines of text, and eats the cookie banner at the bottom. A newsletter pop-up shows up about five seconds later; it eats that too. After each meal, Spit it out (bottom left) puts it back.

Things to try

  • Click the pet to poke it. Poke it ten times and see what happens.
  • Drag it and throw it. It has opinions about that.
  • Press Alt+Shift+W to put it away, and again to bring it back.
  • Open the toolbar popup: start a 25 minute focus session, look at this week's Wrapped, switch eating off for this site.
  • Scroll fast for a while. It notices doomscrolling.

Some words to walk on

Octopuses have three hearts. A group of flamingos is a flamboyance. Honey found in ancient tombs is still edible. Bananas are berries; strawberries are not.

Wombats make cube-shaped droppings. Sea otters hold hands while they sleep. Scotland's national animal is the unicorn. A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus.

There are more possible games of chess than atoms in the observable universe. Sloths can hold their breath longer than dolphins.
